About Us

Farming with Passion

Enock Farm Enterprise has been a trusted name in dairy cattle breeding for over 15 years. Our farm is dedicated to raising healthy, productive dairy cows and bulls that help farmers improve their herds and increase milk production.

We take pride in our sustainable farming practices and our commitment to animal welfare. Our experienced team ensures that all our cattle receive the best care, nutrition, and veterinary attention.

Whether you're looking to start a new dairy operation or improve your existing herd, Enock Farm Enterprise has the quality cattle you need.
